3-Minute Neck & Jaw Decompression (Sir Bhari Relief)

70% of tinnitus cases with head heaviness stem from compressed suboccipital nerves or jaw (TMJ) clenching. Follow these 3 simple stretches:

45s
1

Suboccipital Base Release

Interlock fingers behind the base of your skull. Tuck chin downward gently. Feel the stretch at the top of your neck for 45s.

Relieves pressure on C1/C2 acoustic pathways.
2

TMJ Masseter De-clenching

Place knuckles right under your cheekbones on your jaw muscles. Open mouth slightly and massage downward with gentle pressure.

Relaxes tensor tympani muscle inside ear canal.
3

SCM Side Neck Stretch

Tilt your left ear towards left shoulder, look up towards the right ceiling. Hold and breathe smoothly, then switch sides.

Reduces pulsatile vascular turbulence to ears.
